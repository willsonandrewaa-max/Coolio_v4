01 — Problem

Heat, stickiness, and sweat odor are an everyday problem in the tropics.

Indonesia is hot and humid all year round. On the commute, on campus, at the office, or working outdoors, the body overheats quickly and sweat odor hurts confidence.

Feeling hot & sticky

Hot, humid air makes people uncomfortable, unfocused, and tired faster, especially outdoors and on the commute.

Sweat odor

Sweat clings to skin and clothes. Regular deodorant is made for underarms, not for the whole body or clothing.
